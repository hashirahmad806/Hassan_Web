## Brand & Style

Aura Medical Aesthetic defines the clinical digital presence of Dr. Hassan, Lead Aesthetic Dental Surgeon at ASH Aesthetics (Peshawar). The brand synthesizes two elevated visual worlds:
1. **Cinematic Obsidian Luxury (Hero, Header, CTA, Footer):** Deep, velvety charcoal canvases paired with ambient champagne-gold illumination, subtle surgical video textures, and chiseled hairline borders that communicate elite exclusivity and surgical mastery.
2. **Warm Ivory Clinical Sanctuary (Treatments, The Aura Standard, About, Journey):** Airy, organic porcelain surfaces, warm ivory backdrops, and tranquil typography that evoke wellness, medical precision, and calm confidence.

The emotional posture is bespoke, calm, and authoritative—reassuring high-discretion patients seeking restorative and cosmetic dentistry at the highest tier of international craftsmanship.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Architectural Framing:** Precision hairline borders (1px) in muted gold (`#D0B892`), geometric corner brackets (`.cornerTL`, `.cornerBR`) on clinical portraiture, and subtle diagonal skew guides.
- **Editorial Masking & Typography:** Staggered curtain word reveals on Playfair Display headings, juxtaposing bold serif title weights with soft, sloping italic accents in warm gold.
- **Frosted Materiality (Dual Glassmorphism):** High-opacity obsidian glass for dark navigation strips (`rgba(15, 13, 11, 0.78 - 0.94)`) and warm alabaster glass (`rgba(255, 248, 243, 0.85)`) for clinical stat chips.
- **Living Clinic Telemetry:** Discrete, active patient acceptance indicators featuring emerald green pulses (`#22C55E`) and verified AACD master accreditation hallmarks.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional cadence between editorial elegance and clinical authority.

- **Display & Headings (Playfair Display):**
  - High-contrast editorial serif conveying European bespoke dentistry and luxury medical heritage.
  - Used for hero headlines, section titles, doctor names, and large stat numerals.
  - Standard headings employ `-0.02em` letter spacing with tight, sculpted line heights (1.06 to 1.15).

- **Body & Technical Micro-Copy (Inter):**

  - "Dr. Hassan" brand hallmark rendered in Playfair Display bold with an adjacent 5px glowing champagne dot.
